Before you start

What you need.

  • Your Sovereignty Backup Kit: the PDF and the descriptor file.
  • The hardware devices for one of your vault’s spending paths. Your Backup Kit lists them, and so does the Vault information page in the app.
  • A computer with Liana, a free, open-source desktop wallet, from lianawallet.com.
The steps

Six steps in Liana.

  1. Open Liana and choose “Add an existing Liana wallet”.
  2. Choose a backend: “Use your own node” (your own Bitcoin node or an Electrum server) or “Use Liana Connect” (Wizardsardine’s hosted service).
  3. On “Import the wallet”, choose “Paste a descriptor” and paste the descriptor from your kit exactly, including the parts in square brackets and the checksum after the #.
  4. On “Import seed phrase”, choose Skip. Your keys are on hardware devices.
  5. On “Register descriptor”, connect and unlock each BitBox02 or Jade, select it, and approve on the device’s screen.
  6. Finish setup. Liana shows your balance. To spend, connect the devices for one spending path and sign.

Liana enforces the same rules as Musonius, because both read them from the same descriptor. A path with a waiting period can only be used once its wait is over.
